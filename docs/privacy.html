Privacy

Last updated September 20, 2026.

Your readings stay on your device.

Augury stores saved readings and journal notes locally on your iPhone. We do not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service, or backend for the app.

No personal data collection

Augury does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information, journal entries, card draws, or notes.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Augury only checks whether the unlock is active so it can provide the features you purchased.

On-device reflections

Where available, Augury can provide an optional reflection on a completed reading using Apple’s on-device Foundation Models. The reflection is generated on the device, is not saved by Augury, and is not sent to us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@andypants.xyz.
